# Budget Management

## Overview

Budget management across Amazon Ads products provides advertisers with control over campaign spend through daily budget limits, pacing options, and optimization strategies to maximize return on advertising investment.

## Facts

1. **Daily Budgets** [fact-b9738cb0-f74e0205]: Sponsored Products and Sponsored Display campaigns use daily budget controls that limit maximum spend per day, resetting at midnight each day.

2. **Lifetime Budgets** [fact-b9738cb0-2bf51110]: Sponsored Brands campaigns offer both daily budget options and lifetime budget options for campaigns with fixed duration, allowing advertisers to allocate total spend across the campaign period.

3. **Budget Pacing** [fact-b9738cb0-16587676]: Amazon automatically paces daily budget delivery throughout the day to prevent budgets from depleting too early, ensuring consistent ad delivery during peak shopping hours.

4. **Spend Caps** [fact-b9738cb0-e05450cc]: Campaigns cannot exceed the set daily budget, though actual spend may be slightly higher (up to 10-20%) due to latency in click reporting and budget enforcement.

5. **Budget Recommendations** [fact-b9738cb0-bad44df4]: Amazon provides budget recommendations based on keyword counts, target competition, and historical spend patterns to ensure sufficient budget for campaign effectiveness.

6. **Budget Allocation** [fact-b9738cb0-58058be0]: Advertisers with multiple campaigns can strategically allocate budget across campaigns based on performance, seasonality, and promotional priorities.

7. **Real-Time Budget Monitoring** [fact-b9738cb0-b633f330]: Campaign performance reports show actual spend vs. budget, allowing advertisers to monitor budget utilization and make adjustments during the campaign.

8. **Optimization Strategies** [fact-b9738cb0-b193b9ab]: Effective budget management includes regularly reviewing performance metrics, reallocating budget from underperforming to high-performing campaigns, and adjusting budgets based on seasonal demand.
