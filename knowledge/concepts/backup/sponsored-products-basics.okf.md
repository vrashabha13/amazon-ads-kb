# Sponsored Products Basics

## Overview

Sponsored Products (商品推广) is a cost-per-click (CPC) advertising solution that promotes individual products on Amazon and selected external websites. It helps increase visibility and sales for promoted products.

## Facts

1. **Pricing Model**: Sponsored Products use a cost-per-click (CPC) pricing model where advertisers only pay when customers click their ads.

2. **Ad Formats**: Campaigns can be created using images or interactive long-form videos in minutes, even without advertising experience.

3. **Campaign Creation**: Advertisers can create campaigns by logging into their account, clicking "Create Campaign", and selecting "Sponsored Products".

4. **Customer Journey**: When customers click Sponsored Products ads, they are directed to the promoted product's detail page.

5. **Device Coverage**: Ads appear on desktop browsers, mobile browsers, and Amazon mobile applications.

6. **Stock Availability**: Sponsored Products ads only display when the promoted products are in stock.

7. **Visual Presentation**: Ads showcase multiple product features using images and videos to help customers make informed purchasing decisions.

8. **Creative Assets**: Ads display as images or interactive long-form videos, with video ads showing product usage scenarios.

9. **Video Performance**: Sponsored Products campaigns with video have a 9% higher average click-through rate (CTR) compared to campaigns without video.

10. **New Seller Incentive**: New sellers launching their first Sponsored Products campaign and spending a specified amount within 30 days can receive advertising credits up to $1,000 (availability varies by marketplace).
