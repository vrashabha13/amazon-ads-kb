# Sponsored Brands Basics

## Overview

Sponsored Brands (品牌推广) is a comprehensive advertising solution that helps customers discover brands on Amazon using rich creative assets like videos, images, and collections to attract audience attention and make brands stand out.

## Facts

1. **Creative Formats**: Sponsored Brands support video ads, image collection ads, and can showcase up to three products in a single video ad when linked to brand storefronts.

2. **Ad Placement**: Sponsored Brands appear in prime locations including top of search results首页首位 and product pages, driving traffic to brand storefronts or product detail pages.

3. **Brand Storytelling**: Ads use creative assets including brand logo, custom messaging, and product portfolios to build brand awareness and consideration throughout the customer shopping journey.

4. **Targeting Options**: Campaigns support keyword targeting, product targeting to reach relevant customers through precise targeting methods.

5. **Pricing Models**: Sponsored Brands offer three pricing models: cost-per-click (CPC) for page traffic提升页面效果, cost-per-thousand visible impressions (vCPM) for brand visibility提升品牌展示量份额, and fixed pre-pay pricing for reserving share of voice预留展示份额.

6. **Campaign Goals**: Advertisers can optimize for increasing page visits, improving brand visibility share, or reserving share of voice for predictable top-of-search placements.

7. **Budget Requirements**: CPC and vCPM campaigns have no minimum spend requirements with flexible bid adjustments, while reserved share of voice requires minimum spend commitments.

8. **Measurement**: Campaign performance tracked through impressions, clicks, detail page views, sales metrics, and brand new customer metrics that track first-time brand purchasers over 12-month lookback periods.

9. **AI Creative Tools**: Free AI-powered image generation tools available for creating custom brand creative assets at no additional cost.

10. **Eligibility**: Available to vendors, book vendors, KDP authors, vendors, and professional sellers who have completed Amazon Brand Registry.

11. **Video Services**: Amazon provides video creative creation and editing services, including complimentary videos through qualifying campaigns or affordable paid services.
