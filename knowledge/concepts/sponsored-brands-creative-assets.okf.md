# Sponsored Brands Creative Assets

## Overview

Sponsored Brands offer multiple creative formats designed to help advertisers showcase their brand story, highlight product portfolios, and drive customer engagement through visually compelling ad experiences.

## Facts

1. **Video Ads** [fact-2ddcfdcb-6445fa80]: Sponsored Brands video ads support long-form video content (up to 45 seconds) that showcases brand stories, product features, and lifestyle imagery.

2. **Collection Ads** [fact-2ddcfdcb-d01653cd]: Image collection ads display a brand logo, custom headline, and multiple products in a grid layout, allowing advertisers to showcase product categories or collections.

3. **Single Product Ads** [fact-2ddcfdcb-821636b9]: This format combines a hero image with custom messaging and a single featured product, ideal for promoting specific flagship products.

4. **Creative Specifications** [fact-2ddcfdcb-1c7f7955]: Video ads support 16:9 aspect ratio with recommended resolution of 1920x1080px, while image assets should be high-resolution (minimum 1200x627px).

5. **Logo Requirements** [fact-2ddcfdcb-c62103fc]: Brand logos must be high-resolution with transparent backgrounds for PNG formats or high-quality JPG formats, with recommended size of 400x400px.

6. **Custom Messaging** [fact-2ddcfdcb-6694ad94]: Headlines can include up to 50 characters for collection ads and up to 30 characters for single product ads.

7. **Asset Guidelines** [fact-2ddcfdcb-02939c46]: All creative assets must comply with Amazon's creative acceptance policies including image quality standards, text clarity requirements, and brand representation guidelines.

8. **Lifestyle Imagery** [fact-2ddcfdcb-aeebb565]: Creative assets can include lifestyle photography showing products in use, helping customers visualize product benefits and use cases.
