# Sponsored Brands Basics

## Overview

Sponsored Brands (品牌推广) is a comprehensive advertising solution that helps brands build awareness and consideration through creative, customizable ad formats featuring brand logo, multiple products, and customizable messaging.

## Facts

1. **Creative Formats**: Sponsored Brands support three main creative formats: video ads, image collection ads, and single product ads with custom headlines.

2. **Ad Placement**: Sponsored Brands appear in prominent locations including shopping search results, product detail pages, and Amazon homepage.

3. **Brand Storytelling**: Advertisers can showcase their brand story through creative assets that combine brand logo, custom messaging, and product portfolios.

4. **Targeting Options**: Campaigns can use keyword targeting, product targeting, or audience targeting to reach relevant customers.

5. **Pricing Models**: Sponsored Brands offer flexible pricing including cost-per-click (CPC), cost-per-thousand impressions (vCPM), and fixed pre-pay options.

6. **Campaign Goals**: Advertisers can optimize for different objectives including awareness, consideration, and conversion goals.

7. **Measurement**: Campaign performance is tracked through impressions, clicks, detail page views, and sales metrics with detailed reporting available.

8. **Brand Control**: Advertisers maintain full control over creative assets, brand messaging, and product selection in each ad.
