# Sponsored Products Performance Measurement

## Overview

Performance measurement for Sponsored Products involves tracking key metrics, analyzing campaign effectiveness, and using data-driven insights to optimize advertising spend and improve return on investment.

## Facts

1. **Core Metrics** [fact-b9738cb0-968b49f8]: Sponsored Products campaigns are measured through impressions (ad visibility), clicks (customer engagement), conversions (sales), and spend (advertising cost).

2. **ACoS Calculation** [fact-b9738cb0-54425b25]: Advertising Cost of Sales (ACoS) is calculated as (ad spend ÷ sales revenue) × 100%, showing the percentage of sales revenue spent on advertising.

3. **ROAS Calculation** [fact-b9738cb0-6c09330b]: Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) is calculated as (sales revenue ÷ ad spend), showing revenue generated per dollar spent on advertising.

4. **Click-Through Rate** [fact-b9738cb0-5aadd065]: CTR measures ad engagement by dividing clicks by impressions, helping assess ad relevance and creative effectiveness.

5. **Conversion Rate** [fact-b9738cb0-a7ef563f]: Conversion rate measures purchase likelihood by dividing conversions by clicks, indicating how effectively ads convert browsers to buyers.

6. **Impression Share** [fact-b9738cb0-70ded5dc]: This metric shows the percentage of available impressions that campaigns received, helping identify missed opportunities due to budget or bid constraints.

7. **Search Term Reports** [fact-b9738cb0-97e82212]: Analyzing actual search terms that triggered ads helps identify new keyword opportunities and negative keyword candidates.

8. **Attribution Models** [fact-b9738cb0-9dec4e99]: Amazon uses last-touch attribution, meaning conversions are attributed to the last ad a customer clicked before making a purchase, providing clear campaign impact measurement.
