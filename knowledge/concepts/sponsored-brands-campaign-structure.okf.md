# Sponsored Brands Campaign Structure

## Overview

Sponsored Brands campaigns are organized hierarchically with campaigns containing ad groups, which house individual ads and targeting settings, providing granular control over budget allocation, audience targeting, and creative delivery.

## Facts

1. **Campaign Hierarchy**: Sponsored Brands use a three-level structure: Campaign (budget, schedule, targeting) → Ad Group (ads, bid adjustments) → Ad (creative assets).

2. **Keyword Targeting**: Advertisers can target specific search keywords with match types including broad match, phrase match, and exact match for precise audience control.

3. **Product Targeting**: Campaigns can target specific products or product categories to appear on relevant product detail pages, competing with or complementing specific products.

4. **Audience Targeting**: Sponsored Brands support audience targeting based on shopping interests, lifestyle categories, and life events to reach customers with demonstrated purchase intent.

5. **Budget Controls**: Campaign budgets are set at the campaign level with daily budget limits controlling maximum spend per day, with options for both daily and lifetime budgets.

6. **Bid Management**: Bids can be set at the ad group or keyword level with options for dynamic bidding strategies that automatically adjust based on performance likelihood.

7. **Ad Groups**: Campaigns can contain multiple ad groups, each with different creative assets, targeting settings, and bid strategies to test different approaches.

8. **Campaign Optimization**: Advertisers can use campaign-level settings to optimize for different objectives including awareness (impressions), consideration (detail page visits), or conversion (sales).
