# Bidding Strategies

## Overview

Bidding strategies across Amazon Ads products share common principles of cost-per-click (CPC) pricing while offering product-specific optimizations for different advertising objectives and campaign goals.

## Facts

1. **CPC Pricing Model** [fact-b9738cb0-b27b6985]: All Amazon Ads products (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display) use cost-per-click pricing where advertisers only pay when customers click their ads.

2. **Dynamic Bidding** [fact-b9738cb0-0ed44365]: Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands support dynamic bidding strategies that automatically adjust bids based on the likelihood of conversion, including "dynamic bids - down only," "dynamic bids - up and down," and "fixed bids."

3. **Bid Adjustments** [fact-b9738cb0-5dfcf364]: Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands allow bid adjustments at the ad group or keyword level, enabling advertisers to increase or decrease bids by percentage for specific targets.

4. **Default Bids** [fact-b9738cb0-18f232f6]: Campaigns use default bids at the ad group level, which can be overridden at the keyword or product target level for more granular control.

5. **Bid Strategy Selection** [fact-b9738cb0-6422f92c]: Advertisers can choose bid strategies aligned with campaign objectives: "dynamic bids - down only" for profit optimization, "dynamic bids - up and down" for sales maximization, or "fixed bids" for complete control.

6. **Sponsored Display Bidding** [fact-b9738cb0-855d6b1f]: Sponsored Display offers both CPC and CPM (cost-per-thousand impressions) pricing models, with CPM particularly effective for awareness campaigns where impressions are the primary objective.

7. **Bid Recommendations** [fact-b9738cb0-402073bc]: Amazon provides bid recommendations based on historical performance data, competitive landscape, and estimated likelihood of winning placements.

8. **Performance-Based Bidding** [fact-b9738cb0-eb1d00b8]: All products support performance-based bid optimization that learns from historical campaign performance to adjust bids for targets that drive better results.
