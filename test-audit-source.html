Test Campaign Type

Test Campaign Type is a new advertising format that allows advertisers to promote their products using video creative assets.

Key Features

  • Video Creative: Supports 30-second video advertisements with auto-play functionality
  • Cost-per-impression pricing: Uses CPM pricing model instead of CPC
  • Prime placement: Ads appear on Prime Video and Amazon Live

Requirements

To use Test Campaign Type, advertisers must have a minimum account balance of $500 and maintain professional seller status.
